# Project: Smart Money, ICT & Confluence Trading Platform

## Overview
A comprehensive institutional-grade Forex & Commodities (Gold XAU/USD) technical analysis, automated signal generation, and paper observation system built on:
- **Smart Money Concepts (SMC)**: Break of Structure (BOS), Change of Character (CHoCH), Order Blocks (OB), Fair Value Gaps (FVG), Premium & Discount Arrays.
- **Inner Circle Trader (ICT)**: London & NY Open Killzones, Silver Bullet hours, Optimal Trade Entry (OTE), Judas Swing liquidity sweeps.
- **Fibonacci Multi-Level Engine**: Swing High/Low anchor detection, Golden Pocket (0.618), OTE (0.707/0.786), and extension targets.
- **Session-based Strategies**: Asian Range accumulation, London expansion/sweep, New York continuation/reversal.
- **Liquidity & Stop-Loss Hunt Detection**: BSL/SSL sweeps, Turtle Soup setups, Stop-Loss raids before institutional expansion.
- **Confluence Signal Generator**: Multi-factor scoring engine producing accurate, actionable paper trading signals with entry, invalidation stop-loss, and multi-tier take-profit targets (1:2+ R:R).
- **Interactive Visual Dashboard**: Real-time chart overlays, SMC/ICT analysis panel, live signals feed, session status, and paper trade execution logging.

## Core Target Assets
- Spot Forex Majors: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, USD/CHF, NZD/USD
- Spot Commodities: Gold (XAU/USD / COMEX GC=F)
